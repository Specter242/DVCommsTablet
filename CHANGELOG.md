# Changelog

## 0.1.7 (2026-11-27)

- **VR:** the small buttons are easier to hit. Hold grip with an empty hand
  and point the game's beam at the tablet: the trigger presses the button
  under the beam's dot, not its neighbor. Buttons also take a fingertip a
  little sooner.
- **Inventory icon:** the tablet's icon now matches the game's own icons.

## 0.1.6 (2026-11-24)

- **No hang the first time you take the tablet out:** the other mods' app
  icons are now drawn while the world loads.
- **ROUTE** now says it doesn't throw switches, and Track Ahead counts the
  switches set against the route.
- **SET SWITCHES**, on Track Ahead: throws the switches set against the
  route, as far as its next reversal. Needs the Dispatcher license.
- **Starting kit:** a new Free Roam game starts with a tablet, and so does a
  career started with the Expanded or Engineer starting items. With Basic,
  buy one or use the settings button.

## 0.1.5 (2026-11-21)

- **Spawn** names the locos and cars it leaves out, and why: a license
  you don't hold, or a garage not unlocked.
- **Settings:** Realistic Firing 1.24.3 and Realistic Brakes 1.4.5 put
  their give buttons under Equipment.
- Performance improvements and bug fixes.

## 0.1.4 (2026-11-20)

- **Track Ahead**, a new app: the line ahead of your train on one screen,
  with its grades, speed limits, switches, and the distance and time to
  what comes next. It follows the switches as they are set, or the job of
  the car at the end of your train, or a destination you choose: press
  SET DESTINATION, tap a track on the map, then ROUTE.
- **Map**, a new app on the home screen: the same map as Switches, one tap
  away.
- **Equipment in Settings:** mods that offer them show give buttons at the
  top of their section. Press one and the item lands in front of you.
- **The Settings app always shows** now, with **Give me a comms tablet**
  for a spare.
- **For other mods' authors:** guest contract 3 (`GUEST-APPS.md`) lets any
  mod offer those buttons.

## 0.1.3 (2026-11-17)

- **Double Tracked Valley's switches in the switch books.** With Double
  Track 2.1.1 in its Normal layout, the route map is re-laid to fit its
  passing loops and double track, the station booklet's yard pages get its
  switches, and Steel Mill gets a page for its hump yard. The paper books
  show them, and so does the Switches app's map; with the Dispatcher
  license, press one to throw it. These maps are for Double Tracked Valley:
  they will be removed if its author asks, or once Double Track draws them
  itself. When Double Track or the standalone Double Track Maps already
  draws them, the tablet leaves the books to it.
- **Switches, Map:** on a route map another mod re-lays (Double Track's),
  a press lands on the switch drawn there.
- **For other mods' authors:** an app on the tablet can now aim at a car
  the way Paint does (guest contract 2, in `GUEST-APPS.md`). If DV Car
  Changer adds an app of its own, the Workshop's Change page steps aside
  for it.

## 0.1.2 (2026-11-17)

- **Workshop: Change**, for DV Car Changer 1.5.4. Aim at a car and use
  the tablet, then switch its modifications (a loco's smokestack, pilot,
  headlights, and the like) on and off from a list. One that clashes with
  one already on asks you to press it again to swap. Car Changer saves
  them with your game, as with its radio.
- Change is hidden on Multiplayer clients: only the host can change cars,
  and only the host sees the changes. Another version of Car Changer hides
  it until the tablet is updated for that version.

## 0.1.1 (2026-11-17)

- **Spawn and Paint pictures** now fit every engine to its tile. Some
  custom engines came out tiny or too big and cut off; each picture is now
  framed on the engine itself.
- The pictures now show the doors, handles, and other outside parts.

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-17)

First release: a held tablet that does the comms radio's work on a screen.
Press the round button under the screen for its apps.

- **Vehicles:** spawn locos and cars from a picture of each livery, summon
  crew vehicles in Career, and buy Custom Car Loader's work trains.
- **Cargo:** load and unload the cars you spawned, by car type or from the
  full list.
- **Workshop:** paint a car from pictures of it in each paint (Skin
  Manager's skins when it is installed), and, with Rearranged installed,
  change an S282's or S060's wheel arrangement.
- **Switches:** throw the switch you point at, or open the route map and
  every station's yard maps, with each switch's setting and, with the Map
  Blips setting on, the locomotives. With the Dispatcher license, press a
  switch on the map to throw it.
- **Tools:** rerail, clear, start up, and damage, as on the radio.
- **Light:** the radio's flashlight.
- **Settings:** change the settings of Realistic Firing, AI Engineer,
  Realistic Brakes, and Realistic Stack Effects from the tablet.
- **Mount it** on a cab gadget mount and use it by pointing.
- **Realistic Firing and AI Engineer:** with their updates that know the
  tablet, their pages become its Firing and Engineers apps, and their own
  tablets are retired: a saved one becomes a Comms Tablet.
- Sold beside the comms radio in shops; its settings window has a button
  that gives you one.
