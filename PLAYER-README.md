# Comms Tablet

A held tablet that does the comms radio's work on a screen. Instead of
stepping through long lists one press at a time on the radio, you choose
on the tablet (with a picture of each vehicle or paint), then aim and use
the tablet just as you would the radio.

## Getting one

- Buy it in the shops, beside the comms radio.
- Or press **Give me a comms tablet** in the mod's settings (Unity Mod
  Manager, Ctrl+F10).
- With Realistic Firing or AI Engineer installed (their versions that know
  the tablet), the starting kit gives you one, once per save.

## Using it

- Hold it like any item. On a desktop, use mouse mode (as with other held
  items) to press its screen; click away from the screen, or use the
  tablet, to aim and confirm as with the radio. The scroll wheel steps
  where the radio's buttons would.
- The round button under the screen goes to the home screen of apps.
- An app opens on the page you last used in it.

## The apps

- **Vehicles:** Crew (summon your crew vehicles; Career), Buy (Custom Car
  Loader's work trains; Career with CCL), and Spawn (locos and cars, a
  picture of each livery).
- **Cargo:** load and unload the cars you spawned.
- **Workshop:** Paint (aim at a car first, then pick from pictures of it in
  each paint; Skin Manager's skins when installed) and Wheels (Rearranged's
  S282 and S060 wheel arrangements, when Rearranged is installed).
- **Switches:** Switch (point at a switch and use the tablet) and Map (the
  route map and every station's yard maps, with each switch's setting; the
  locomotives show with the game's Map Blips setting; with the Dispatcher
  license, press a switch to throw it).
- **Tools:** Rerail, Clear, Startup, and Damage, as on the radio.
- **Light:** switches the tablet's flashlight on and off.
- **Settings:** the settings of the author's other mods that are installed
  (Realistic Firing, AI Engineer, Realistic Brakes, Realistic Stack
  Effects), saved just as their own settings windows save them.
- **Firing** and **Engineers:** Realistic Firing's and AI Engineer's pages,
  with their versions that know the tablet.

Apps appear only where the radio would offer them: Spawn, Cargo, Startup,
and Damage need the comms radio's cheat setting, and so does Paint unless
Skin Manager allows it (its own setting, on by default); Crew is a Career
thing.

## Mounting it

Aim at a free gadget mount in a cab and click to put the tablet on it.
Mounted, point at the screen and click to use it; the apps that aim (Spawn,
Paint, Rerail, and the like) ask you to take it in hand.

## Optional mods

- **Skin Manager:** the Paint page shows its skins.
- **Custom Car Loader:** its engines in Spawn, its work trains in Buy.
- **Rearranged S282 / S060:** the Wheels page.
- **Realistic Firing, AI Engineer:** their apps (Firing, Engineers), and
  their own tablets retired.

## Multiplayer

Every player needs the mod. It has not been tried in Multiplayer yet; the
radio's work it does goes through the game's own code, as the radio's
does, and Multiplayer's checks apply to it. Engineers is hidden on
clients unless AI Engineer allows driving there.

## Removing it

A tablet saved in your game disappears if you remove the mod. While
Realistic Firing or AI Engineer is merged with it, their own tablets in
that save have become Comms Tablets, so removing this mod loses those too.
Realistic Firing and AI Engineer then work on their own again. From
Realistic Firing 1.24.0 and AI Engineer 0.15.1 on, their starting kits
give their tablets once more at the next load; you can also buy them in
the shops, or press F9 for AI Engineer's.
